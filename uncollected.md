# Understanding the Uncollected

Over 11000 of the tracks in Voca are tagged 'uncollected.' These poems may have been published in other places, but have not, to the best of the taggers' knowledge, been published as part of a poetry collection. 

These uncollected poems make Voca as a resource even more unique. Although poetry is increasingly published online, it is not always easy to find individually published poems. Digital magazines and journals may go out of print, move platforms, or become otherwise less discoverable. Voca, then, provides an open digital space where people can hear poems that might be challenging to find elsewhere.
