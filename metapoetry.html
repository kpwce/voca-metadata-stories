Making Metapoetry

Written by Alysa Meng and Maryelizabeth Koepele

Poetry's Uncomputability

Alysa Meng

I scraped the Voca collection one evening.

An audiovisual allure brimming with tantalizing findings.

Jackpot! I thought that night.

What a perfect dataset to glean insight!

Promised metadata paradises to decrypt,

Using a ten-minute programming script,

thousands of college poetry readings,

Machine-readable text for statistical processing.

But then a typo—"san fransisco."

A link clicked—missing captions and audio.

Cambodia's krama, intangible cultural heritage of a nation,

becomes Mexico's crema, due to transcription automation.

Classifications and visualizations

reimagine search and computation.

Should we lower cognitive load

by transforming metaphors into binary code?

When static labels reduce a dynamic space,

What identities do we erase?

Whose voices remain unheard?

Who really interprets these precious words?

It's not a question of time complexity.

The archive resists computability.

After all, computers cannot quantify our humanity.

INFORMATION IMPLICATION

Maryelizabeth Koepele

Abandonment’s a worse aesthetic when it’s digital; creeping plants won’t grow

Babies through cracks in broken links & anyway what people want is photographs of rare

Cacti or short-lived butterflies even bright & muddy plastic pesticide containers! Is digital data

Dada? Or is the screen too braided up with symbolism? Plus the hazards, like click a rifle-laden

Eagle & maybe it’s downloading an historical evil or gambling app. So this is where the

Fables live now & art, more interconnected & perhaps less fragile than before. Available for

Gangs of corporate bots & aunts, grandmothers, brothers. Too expansive for a

Haibun but glossed with standardization. Hand lettering leaving; each word’s a god again like mint

Ice cream in the Tucson heat & you can eat it! What longings are left in the whitespaces or

Jank dead blogs by midwest teens? We won’t say

Kaddish for some lost bytes. This is a poem for dynamic data, still evolving, deep listening

Labor of attention! Here is meditation attuned to

Macaronic expression, to metaphors, to meter. To find a

Name for every category’s a parable of minor power– the font suggests

Objectivity but this heap of words is romance.

Pain and all of it. My own computer heats & whirs when I listen too long. A

Q and A on the Apple forum tells me I’ve abused my storage. Outside

Rabbits run around the creosote. I’m headphones in 2001 yes there’s

Sadness & two sets of poems depending on september’s capitalization. Were there

Tabloids stealing grief? I was too young & still don’t know proper

Ultratranslation to layer past & present. The poets don’t say who sold

Vacations for survivors. I’ll leave the rabbits and the creosote. The audio

Waffles. My technology’s decaying but I’m still building data. More

eXactly: something’s building mine & someone’s harvesting & maybe hoping

Young readers won’t learn scaffolding if the structure’s smooth enough. But there was a lone

Zebra in the local zoo & she hasn’t been forgotten.
